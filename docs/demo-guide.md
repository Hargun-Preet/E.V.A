# Demo guide

Ideas and tips for recording E.V.A.

## Demos

| Demo | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Wave hello** | Gestures → *Wave hello*, or import [`poses/hi_wave_poses.json`](../poses/hi_wave_poses.json) | Best at speed 50–70. A good intro or outro |
| **Pick & place a paper cube** | *Pick & place* card | The star demo. Paper is light and forgiving |
| **Gestures** | Nod, shake, bow, point, handshake | Quick filler between the main shots |
| **Stack cubes** | Run the pick & place sequence twice with different drop heights | Raise *Drop height* by one cube height for the second cube |
| **Digital twin side by side** | Screen recording of the 3D view next to the real arm | Shows that the twin mirrors the real angles |

## Setting up the cube pick

1. Mark the cube position with tape (an L shape) so every take starts the same.
2. Put the cube about 13–18 cm in front of the base, inside the green band on the map.
3. Click the cube and the drop spot on the map; press **Check sequence**.
4. Tune **Grip angle** on the real cube first: firm, but the gripper servo must not buzz.
5. Do the first run at speed 20–30%, then raise the speed for the final takes.
6. If it lands 1 cm off, use the **trim** fields instead of re-teaching.

## Filming tips

- Plain background, good light, camera on a stand. Film the arm and the dashboard in the same shot.
- Slow, deliberate motion looks better on camera than fast motion.
- Do two or three takes. A good 40-second storyboard: home → wave → pick cube 1 → place → pick cube 2 → stack → home.
- Keep a hand near the power switch for every take.

## Before you publish

- [ ] **Privacy:** check the background of every photo and video. Remove or blur people, family photos, addresses and anything else you would not want online.
- [ ] Do not commit large videos to git. Upload to YouTube (or a GitHub Release) and link them from the README with a thumbnail.
- [ ] Keep GIFs short (under about 10 MB) and put them in [`media/`](../media/README.md).
- [ ] Mention the safety notes if you show the wiring.
