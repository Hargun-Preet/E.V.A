## Rules of thumb

- **Check the background** of every photo and video for people, family photos, addresses or anything private. Crop, blur or retake.
- Keep images under about 1–2 MB (resize to 1600 px wide); do not commit videos to git. Upload them to YouTube or a GitHub Release and link them.
- Use descriptive file names in lower case with hyphens, for example `eva-home-pose.jpg`.
